Debrief: talk it over

IB connection: Responsibility · Relationships · Ethics

  1. Responsibility: Which of your own habits would the Hex-Hacker have used? What will you change this week?
  2. Relationships: A friend asks for your password "to be nice". What do you say? How does sharing it affect trust?
  3. Ethics: You find a classmate's account left logged in. What should you do, and why?
  4. Think bigger: Metadata revealed who, when and where. Is it fair for a school to track this? Where is the line between safety and privacy?
  5. Technology understanding: Why is a long passphrase stronger than a short complicated password? What does "key" mean in encryption?
